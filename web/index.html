Chicago
Loop

A 1:1 model of the Loop and the elevated L, built in Blender from OpenStreetMap and City of Chicago data. Brown, Green, Orange, Pink and Purple Line trains move on the real track.

There is no CTA Train Tracker key yet, so the trains are simulated in the exact format of CTA's feed, on real paths and timetables. Switching to live positions is one setting.
